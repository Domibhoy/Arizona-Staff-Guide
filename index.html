SEC-01 // WELCOME

Welcome to the team

This guide is the single source of truth for how staff operate, enforce rules, and grow within the server. New trainee or veteran administrator — you're expected to know it.

Our mission is a realistic, fair, and enjoyable ER:LC experience for every player. That starts with staff who are professional, consistent, and approachable.

Read this guide in full, keep it bookmarked, and ask leadership if anything is unclear.

SEC-02 // CHAIN OF COMMAND

Ranks

Authority and responsibility rise together. Each tier below carries more of both than the last.

T1
Trainee
  • Observe and learn from experienced staff
  • Handle basic questions and assist players
  • Do not issue major punishments unsupervised
T2
Moderator
  • Enforce server rules consistently
  • Issue warnings and kicks
  • Handle in-game player reports
T3
Senior Moderator
  • Oversee patrols and RP quality
  • Assist with escalations
  • Lead by example for all staff
T4
Administrator
  • Oversee daily operations
  • Manage bans, unbans, and major incidents
  • Assist with policy decisions
T5
High Command
  • Set server policy and direction
  • Manage staff promotions and demotions
  • Final say on all appeals and conflicts

SEC-06 // ON SCENE

Responding to mod calls

Have a speech ready before you respond. Something like:

RADIO TRANSCRIPT

"Hello, my name is [username], I am a staff member for Arizona Roleplay. How can I assist you?"

  • Never ignore or void a mod call.
  • Park your vehicle properly before responding.
  • Once resolved, ask "Anything else?" before closing out.
  • Witnesses and statements aren't reliable evidence on their own.
  • Hear all sides before deciding — skipping this can lead to punishment for you.
SEC-07 // TOOLING

Staff commands

Available commands vary by rank and admin system. Check yours before you rely on it.

# list your available commands
:cmds
# log a punishment
:log punishment username reason → kick | warn | ban
  • Never use a command you aren't authorised for.
  • Commands are for legitimate moderation or admin purposes only.
  • "All" / "others" / multi-player commands need high-rank permission first — misuse may mean a blacklist.
  • Shutdowns require sign-off from an Assistant Director or higher.
  • Command abuse can lead to disciplinary action.
  • Use at least 5 letters of a username in commands — fewer counts as LLC and may be an infraction.
SEC-08 // PROCEDURE

Discord checking

Run at least once per shift. In the Staff Commands Channel, run /erlc membercheck — an X means that user isn't in the Discord.

Bring flagged users to the Springfield cinema (theater #1 or #2) and block the entrance/exit with crowd fences or barricades — traffic cones don't hold.

View the user to confirm they're not in a mod scene or driving.

If they're driving, private message them to pull over so their car isn't mid-road.

If they don't pull over, use :jail — this deletes their vehicle.

Use the bring command — you may need it more than once if they're seated in a vehicle.

Once teleported, say: "Hello, you are not in the comms server. Please join it."

Traffic cones do not block movement here — only crowd fences or barricades work for this procedure.

SEC-10 // STANDARDS

Staff conduct

The expectations that don't show up in a punishment table but matter just as much.

Be respectful to all players regardless of rank or behavior.

Stay professional in every server and Discord channel.

Never abuse staff powers for personal roleplay or advantage.

Enforce rules consistently and without bias.

Never leak staff information, logs, or player data.

Handle reports promptly — don't leave players waiting.

Admit mistakes and correct them when they happen.

Stay in character on duty, except when actively enforcing.

SEC-11 // CLOSING

Appeals

Appeals may only be filed for punishments not marked unappealable. A Senior Moderator or higher reviews the evidence, discusses with the issuing staff member if needed, and makes the final call. Keep appeals factual and respectful.

Staff must be familiar with this guide before taking any administrative action — ignorance of policy is never an excuse for inconsistent enforcement. When in doubt, escalate before you act.

By representing Arizona Roleplay as a staff member, you agree to uphold these standards, protect the player experience, and lead by example at all times.
